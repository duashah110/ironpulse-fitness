Questions

Is the first session really free?

Yes. Book a trial, come in, and a coach will show you around and train with you. No payment needed.

I am a complete beginner. Is that okay?

Absolutely. Most of our members started as beginners. Coaches teach you the basics step by step.

Do you have separate timings for women?

Yes, we run dedicated women-only classes. Message us on WhatsApp for the schedule.

Can I freeze or cancel my membership?

You can pause for up to 30 days or cancel any time with a short notice.
